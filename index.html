A quick word first

Wireframes, not the finished site.

These are the structure and flow of each page, not the built site and not the final design. Grey boxes stand in for product images, and the brand look comes later. The product names, specs and prices are pulled from your live site. Each page mirrors what you run today, and only a few moments are elevated.

The discipline

Lift and shift, not a rebuild.

We are not redrawing your site. Most of every page is exactly what you run today, rebuilt in the new system. We elevate only a few moments, the ones where a small change makes a real difference. Two things are genuinely new, because you do not have them today: a real search, and product compare.

How people move

Four ways buyers move through the site.

Flow 1 · about 70% of volume

Find and buy

Arrive, find the exact part by search or category, confirm it fits, add to cart, check out.

Flow 2 · the B2B lead

Volume quote

A business or reseller submits a bulk quote without an account, on one sector-aware form.

Flow 3 · the repeat buyer

Bulk reorder

A signed-in business buyer pastes a part-number list and orders in seconds.

Flow 4 · the browse markets

Research, then reseller

In the 22 non-selling markets, the buyer researches and is routed to an authorized reseller.
